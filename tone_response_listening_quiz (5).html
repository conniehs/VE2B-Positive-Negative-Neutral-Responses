POSITIVE CADENCE

Rising Pitch & Warm Dynamics

Characterized by higher fundamental pitch, melodic variations, and energetic stress on enthusiastic adjectives (e.g., "That sounds wonderful!").

NEUTRAL CADENCE

Monotone & Objective

Steady pacing with minimal inflection or pitch variation, conveying factual statements without emotional bias (e.g., "I see what you mean.").

NEGATIVE CADENCE

Falling Pitch & Hesitation

Lower key registers, abrupt stops, hesitant pauses, or sharp downward pitch glides (e.g., "I really don't think so.").
